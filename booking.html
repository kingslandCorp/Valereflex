This form checks availability against the slots shown, then prepares an email to Kim with everything filled in — nothing gets lost in translation. For a live, always-up-to-date calendar that clients can book instantly (and that syncs with Kim's diary), this can be connected to a booking platform such as Wix Bookings, Fresha or Acuity.
