How this page works

Live content, one-click sharing

This page automatically searches for recent reflexology and holistic wellbeing news each time it's opened, so there's always something current to talk about — no writing needed. It's designed to be checked once a week (a two-minute job) to pick a story and share it.

  • Share to Facebook opens Facebook's own share window, pre-filled with the story — genuinely posts to whichever Facebook account or Page Kim is logged into, in one click.
  • Copy caption for Instagram copies a ready-made caption with hashtags to the clipboard. Instagram doesn't allow websites to post directly, so the last step is opening the Instagram app and pasting it into a new post — this is a limit Instagram places on every website, not something specific to this site.

Want it to post automatically, with no weekly click at all? That's possible, but it needs a Meta Business/Developer account connected to a small scheduling backend (tools like Buffer, Later or Zapier can do this without custom code). Just say the word and this can be planned out and wired up.
