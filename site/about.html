Duopody — a two-year clinical study

Unlike a "nice" foot rub offered in a hotel or spa, Duopody reflexology is a clinical technique studied intensively over two years. Both feet are treated at the same time, with each system in the body reflected in the feet — and each foot able to represent pairs in life, such as past or present.

Kim advises any client booking reflexology to check their practitioner is meaningfully qualified (HND Level 5 minimum) — every reflexologist should be dedicated to the highest quality of care.
